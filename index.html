How to Get There

By Car:

Via Nicoll Highway: Proceed along Nicoll Highway towards Raffles Boulevard. Make a U-turn at the Bras Basah Road Junction. Continue forward and turn left into South Beach.

Via Bras Basah Road: Proceed along Bras Basah Road, then turn left onto Nicoll Highway. Continue forward and turn left into South Beach.

Complimentary parking tickets available at the venue.

By MRT:

Via Esplanade MRT Station: Exit through exit F of Esplanade MRT station

Via City Hall MRT Station: Take the Citylink Mall underpass to exit F of Esplanade MRT station

By Bus:

Along Beach Road: Bus Stop 01619 - Services 56, 57, 100, 107, 107M, 961

Along Nicoll Highway: Bus Stop 80151 - Services 10, 10e, 14, 14e, 16, 70, 70M, 196, 196e
